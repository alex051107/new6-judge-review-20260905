Dataset LI01 Regional labour market: local indicators for counties, local and unitary authorities

Corrections and notices

Corrections

23 July 2026

Because of a processing issue, employment, unemployment and economic inactivity levels and rates for the countries of Wales and Scotland were incorrect in the July 2026 original release of datasets LI01, LI02, LI02.1 (Scotland only) and LI04, for the period April 2025 to March 2026. These data have now been corrected. We apologise for this error.

Notices

14 October 2025

A link for LI02.1 Regional labour market: local indicators for constituencies of the Scottish Parliament was originally published, which directed users to the incorrect dataset. The link has now been corrected. We are sorry for any inconvenience caused.

About this Dataset

Labour market indicators for local authorities, unitary authorities, counties and regions in Great Britain for a 12-month period, published quarterly. These are official statistics in development.
