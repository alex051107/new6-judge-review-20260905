# Reporting policy — project-authored analysis request
Source: UCI Online Retail, dataset 352. The reporting window is 2011-10-01 00:00 inclusive to 2011-11-01 00:00 exclusive, using the timestamps as supplied. The extract also contains adjacent-day records; these must be accounted for as outside scope.
Each physical source row is one occurrence, identified by source_row_id. Preserve repeat-looking occurrences; do not infer duplicate transmission from equal business fields. Use signed Quantity × UnitPrice in GBP.
Retain every source occurrence in the delivered classified analysis, including adjacent-day occurrences explicitly marked outside the reporting window. The register may be divided across sheets. Outside-window amounts must remain separate from October trading totals.
A sales entry has positive Quantity and positive UnitPrice without a C-prefixed invoice. A credit/cancellation entry has negative Quantity, positive UnitPrice and a C-prefixed invoice (case-insensitive). Other sign/identity combinations, absent essential fields and nonpositive prices are exceptions; retain the recorded amount when it is calculable and label its treatment. Missing CustomerID alone does not exclude an otherwise valid sale or credit: report it separately as an attribution issue.
Essential business fields for this review are InvoiceNo, StockCode, Description, Quantity, InvoiceDate, UnitPrice and Country. An empty description is therefore an exception. Retain separate sales, credit and exception subtotals within invoice and country summaries so their amounts and occurrence counts can be reconciled.
Report sales and credits separately; do not claim that the resulting recorded value is audited revenue or cash received. Treat each source invoice identifier as its own transaction document. No credit-to-original matching is authorized without supplied linkage evidence.
