# Workpaper conventions
Use the October 2024 Order of Cost Estimate, Revision A, for the Municipal Building, The Moor, Falmouth. Keep its elemental breakdown, summary chain, provisional sums, exclusions and qualifications reviewable against the source. The review correspondence sets the current review position.

Preserve the printed amounts and rates as source facts. Provisional sums already included in elemental costs are memorandum detail; do not assume an allocation the documents do not establish. A source dash, exclusion or unpriced entry retains its meaning.

Retain full precision in working calculations and display GBP rounded to whole pounds. Reconcile the current cost limit to the original printed estimate, keeping any rounding differences visible. You may also compare against a recalculated original position when that alternative basis and amount are clearly shown; retain the printed source figures.

Prices and allowance percentages will be edited within the stated review scope. An option awaiting approval remains outside the current cost limit when only its price changes. The result remains an estimate with its source limitations.
