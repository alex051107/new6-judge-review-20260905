# Pricing scope and test-request conventions
Use Notice 123 effective July 12, 2026, printed page 5 (Priority Mail Retail) and page 7 (USPS Ground Advantage Retail). Use zones 1–8 only. Extract Priority Mail bands from 1 through 10 pounds and Ground Advantage's ounce bands through 15.999 oz plus its 1–10 pound bands, including every zone price in that scope. Respect the published upper-bound semantics and the declared weight unit.
The attached requests are project-authored test quote requests, not observed customer shipments. Requested zones are already supplied. Every parcel uses the sender's own ordinary rectangular carton, no USPS flat-rate packaging; length at most 20 inches, volume at most 1,000 cubic inches and length plus girth at most 90 inches. None has nonstandard characteristics, hazardous/perishable/live contents, extra services or special origin/destination treatment. No commercial, flat-rate, oversized or Alaska/Limited Overland Route tariff applies.
Weights are positive and at most 10 pounds. Quote both services from their respective grids, choose the lower price (Ground Advantage on an exact tie) and sum chosen prices. Outside these conditions, report out-of-scope; do not guess postage. Retain 16 ounces per pound when interpreting requests. Dynamic changes stay within this declared scope.
