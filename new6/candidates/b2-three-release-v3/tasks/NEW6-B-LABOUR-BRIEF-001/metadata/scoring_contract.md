# B2 three-release scoring contract

The candidate performs an update of a reconstructed earlier briefing using three official releases. January 2023 is the corrected file linked by the official January dataset page; its internal cover says February 2023. The observation periods are October 2021–September 2022, October 2022–September 2023 and October 2023–September 2024. Source geography codes, definitions and missing values determine comparability.

All facts come from the submitted workbook or preserved post-run input files. The Decimal Oracle independently recomputes the reference from the original sources and verifies them against a separate OOXML reader. The reference builder uses integer tenths in JavaScript and checks parity after computing its results. The candidate reader discovers labelled tables and chart references without using Oracle values to choose a region.

## Fixed points before the pilot

| Item | Capability first | Balanced | Ongoing use | Observable business evidence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| R001 | 15 | 20 | 10 | Correct periods, definitions, source identities, percentage-point units, English scope, sampling limits and explanations for unassessable authorities |
| R002 | 25 | 20 | 20 | Current up-to-five selection is complete, unique and correctly ranked under both consecutive comparisons |
| R003 | 25 | 25 | 20 | Supporting figures for current selections and departures agree with published rates; other displayed numerical claims are correct |
| R004 | 20 | 20 | 25 | Current charts, entry/exit explanations and briefing agree with the updated findings |
| R005 | 15 | 15 | 25 | Previous analysis remains reviewable and source evidence remains available |
| Total | 100 | 100 | 100 | One fact vector, three weighting profiles |

Only the unrounded score of at least 0.70 passes. There are no additional hurdles, caps or separate hidden penalties. Missing or malformed output after a normally completed run scores zero; an unresolved parser or engine limitation remains pending. Normal native calculation of a static task occurs in an isolated copy whenever formulas exist; the original answer is read-only.

R001 averages seven business buckets: three observation periods, two population definitions, three source identities, units, scope, sampling limitations, and reasons/no imputation for each of the 23 authorities lacking a complete comparison. Exact filenames or tab names are not mandatory source citations. R002 averages multiset completeness and the five ranks, checking every current final representation; physical row order can differ when explicit ranks retain meaning.

R003 assigns 80% to 33 supporting numerical facts: five changes per current shortlisted authority, and two second-interval changes for each of the four authorities leaving the previous shortlist. Explicit changes or differences computed from candidate-displayed rates can support a fact. The remaining 20% checks whether additional displayed rates/changes contain a contradiction. Merely supplying more rows cannot dilute a required missing or incorrect fact. The full 313-authority matrix in the reference is an accepted layout, not a private mandatory output format.

R004 assigns 45% to chart evidence (half to coverage of the current five, half to correctness of every displayed point, stated scope and cache), 40% to the nine entry/exit/retained explanations, and 15% to the current briefing and its stated numerical claims. Different chart layouts and series combinations are valid. Historical charts remain historical. R005 checks availability of the preserved previous analysis, the correctness of embedded historical claims, and preservation of source files. A clear reference to the retained original previous workbook is accepted in place of duplicated historical worksheets.

Reader limitations are explicit. Unbound authority tables, unresolved formula caches and unsupported chart references are pending with evidence. They do not establish Agent business failure. The calibration suite includes valid formulas/static layouts and targeted errors with explicit lost/preserved criterion assertions; fixture filenames never determine a pass result.

## Actual-answer compatibility in Judge v3.1

Geography codes take precedence over names in prose; name fallback uses complete words. Cambridge cannot bind Cambridgeshire. An explicit current-exclusion reason can explain a departure from the previous shortlist without a private movement-column requirement. Unrecognized genuine narrative remains pending; demonstrated missing output remains a scored omission.

Numeric claims are compared at the public one-decimal precision. Additional stored precision is accepted when it rounds to the same declared business quantity; no algorithm is prescribed. The Oracle's eligibility and ranking still use the published one-decimal rates. A small raw positive change that becomes zero under the public rule cannot qualify an authority.

Historical availability has five fixed components: data, shortlist, brief, exclusions and chart. A retained externally referenced original workbook supplies all five; an embedded partial history earns its actually retained share. Missing the old chart does not erase credit for four preserved historical components. Historical correctness and original-source protection remain separate checks.

The original new-task pilot used Judge v3.0 and returned a parser pending state. The exact same answer is scored under v3.1 after ten focused compatibility checks. No new Agent call, new task obligation or profile weight change was introduced. These remain development results; formal difficulty, external negative-item acceptance and general agentic parsing acceptance are not established.
