# Quotation scope
Use Notice 123 effective July 12, 2026 for Priority Mail Retail and USPS Ground Advantage Retail, zones 1–8. The rate sheet should cover every zone price for Priority Mail's 1–10 pound bands and Ground Advantage's ounce bands through 15.999 oz and 1–10 pound bands.

The supplied quotation requests were created for this reconstruction exercise. Each request supplies its zone. Parcel weights are positive and no more than 10 pounds.

Every parcel uses the sender's own ordinary rectangular carton, with no USPS flat-rate packaging. Length is at most 20 inches, volume at most 1,000 cubic inches, and length plus girth at most 90 inches. The parcels have no nonstandard characteristics, hazardous, perishable or live contents, or extra services. Use regular domestic retail pricing, without special origin/destination treatment or Alaska/Limited Overland Route pricing.

If both services have the same price, select Ground Advantage. Weight and zone edits will remain within these conditions.
