# Local labour-market review
Using the two supplied LI01 releases and the reporting scope in `review_brief.md`, prepare a comparison of English local-authority employment and unemployment outcomes.

Return `/app/output/answer.xlsx` with a comparable-area dataset, rate changes, a record of exclusions, a chart and a short briefing. Identify up to five comparable authorities with the largest unemployment-rate increases and show their employment changes alongside them. Use the source definitions and observation periods, and make the figures and qualifications traceable to the releases.
